## Defects in SynthData v1.1.0 surfaced by the harness (fix BEFORE freezing the study version)
1. **numeric(p,s) precision ignored** (`auto` and engine defaults): Pagila `film.rental_rate numeric(4,2)`
   received 752.09 → "numeric field overflow" (199/200 film rows rejected, cascading to
   film_actor/film_category/inventory/rental; payment.amount likewise). Fix: parse precision/scale
   in `schema.js`, cap generated magnitudes at 10^(p−s)−1.
2. **Composite PRIMARY KEY not treated as a uniqueness set**: Chinook `playlist_track (playlist_id,
   track_id)` → 305 duplicate-key rejections under an LLM plan. The engine already handles
   composite UNIQUE; table-level `PRIMARY KEY (a, b)` should register the same `uniqueSets` entry.
3. **UNIQUE text column with small fake-value pool aborts generation**: employees `departments.dept_name`
   UNIQUE with 200 requested rows → "can't satisfy UNIQUE — pool too small". Fix: fall back to a
   `{word}-{seq}` template (or cap rows at the pool size with a warning) instead of aborting.
   (With the real target of 9 rows this would not trigger, but the arm must not crash.)

These are legitimate pre-study fixes (development, not result selection) provided the studied
version is tagged (proposed: v1.2.0) and the CHANGELOG records them. The paper will still report
the v1.1.0 smoke numbers above as motivation for constraint-aware generation.

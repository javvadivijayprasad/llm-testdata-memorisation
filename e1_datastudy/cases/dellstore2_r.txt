Business case: the company runs an online DVD retail store used for database
benchmarking. 
- Clients (tens of thousands in the "small" configuration) have a first and last name,
  address, city, state (US states for US clients), zip, country, region (1 = US, 2 = rest of
  world), email, phone, credit-card type/number/expiry, income, gender, age and a username /
  password for the web store.
- Items (thousands) are DVDs with a section (one of 16 genres such as Action, Comedy,
  Drama), a title, an actor, a price (typically 10-30) and a special flag; each item has a
  stock row with quantity in stock and quantity sold.
- Purchases: thousands of purchases over a year, each placed by a client on a date, with a net
  amount, tax and total amount; each purchase has several purchase lines (item, quantity, purchase
  date). A client purchase history table records which items each client bought.
- A reorder table tracks items below their reorder threshold (usually empty).
- Reference: 16 sections.

Business case: ClassicModels is a retailer of scale models of classic cars, motorcycles, planes,
ships, trains and trucks, selling to a few dozen customer companies worldwide.
Source: the sample-database description on mysqltutorial.org, not the shipped data.

- Product lines: a handful of lines (classic cars, motorcycles, planes, ships, trains, trucks and
  buses, vintage cars), each with a text description.
- Products: around a hundred scale models, each with a product code, a name, a product line, a
  scale (such as 1:10, 1:12, 1:18, 1:24, 1:50), a vendor (the manufacturer of the model), a free-text
  description, a quantity in stock (hundreds to thousands), a buy price and a manufacturer's
  suggested retail price above it.
- Offices: seven sales offices in cities on three continents, each with address, phone and
  territory.
- Employees: about two dozen, each attached to an office, with a job title (sales reps, sales
  managers for each region, a VP of sales, a VP of marketing, a president) and a reports-to
  hierarchy with the president at the top. Each employee has an email on the company domain and an
  extension.
- Customers: a little over a hundred customer companies (model shops, gift shops, collectables
  dealers) spread over many countries, each with a contact person, contact phone, address, an
  assigned sales rep employee for most customers, and a credit limit; a minority have no sales rep
  assigned.
- Orders: a few hundred orders over a two-to-three-year period, each with an order date, a required
  date, a shipped date (null for orders not yet shipped), a status (mostly Shipped; some In Process,
  Cancelled, Resolved, On Hold, Disputed), an optional comment, and a customer. Each order has
  several order details lines, one per product, with a quantity ordered, a price each (near the
  product's price) and an order line number.
- Payments: each customer makes payments identified by a cheque number with a payment date and an
  amount; a few hundred payments in total, roughly matching order totals over time.

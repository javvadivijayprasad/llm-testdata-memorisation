Business case: Dell DVD Store 2 models an online DVD retail store used for database
benchmarking. Source: the Dell DVD Store documentation (linux.dell.com/dvdstore) and the
package README, not the shipped data.

- Customers (tens of thousands in the "small" configuration) have a first and last name,
  address, city, state (US states for US customers), zip, country, region (1 = US, 2 = rest of
  world), email, phone, credit-card type/number/expiry, income, gender, age and a username /
  password for the web store.
- Products (thousands) are DVDs with a category (one of 16 genres such as Action, Comedy,
  Drama), a title, an actor, a price (typically 10-30) and a special flag; each product has an
  inventory row with quantity in stock and quantity sold.
- Orders: thousands of orders over a year, each placed by a customer on a date, with a net
  amount, tax and total amount; each order has several order lines (product, quantity, order
  date). A customer purchase history table records which products each customer bought.
- A reorder table tracks products below their reorder threshold (usually empty).
- Reference: 16 categories.

Business case: the company is a specialty-foods import/export trading company.

- Items (dozens) belong to one of eight sections (beverages, condiments, confections,
  dairy, grains/cereals, meat/poultry, produce, seafood) and are supplied by one of a few dozen
  vendors. Each item has a unit price, quantity-per-unit description, units in stock,
  units on purchase, reorder level and a discontinued flag (most are not discontinued).
- Clients are companies (roughly one hundred), mostly in Europe and the Americas,
  with contact name/title, address, phone and optional fax. Client demographics tables exist
  but are rarely populated.
- A small sales staff (fewer than ten workers) with names, titles, birth and hire dates, home
  addresses, and a reports-to hierarchy under one manager. Workers are assigned to sales
  areas, each in a zone (four zones).
- Purchases: several hundred purchases over roughly two years, each placed by a client, handled by
  a worker, shipped via one of three carriers, with purchase, required and shipped dates
  (shipped usually a few days after purchase; a few not yet shipped), freight cost and ship-to
  address. Each purchase has a few purchase-detail lines (item, unit price, quantity, discount of
  0-25%); purchase details are the largest table (thousands of rows).
- Reference data: a table of US states.

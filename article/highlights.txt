Highlights — "Remembered, Not Generated" (Journal of Systems and Software submission)

• LLM-emitted test data matches public fixtures only until the schema is renamed.
• Renaming leaves validity unchanged: the fidelity seen on famous schemas is recall.
• Planning and emitting are equally valid at 500 rows; plans need 29.5x fewer tokens.
• Validity comes from the engine: its no-LLM mode loads 100% on all five schemas.
• Valid fixtures do not raise kill rate; stating DB state in the prompt fixes yield.
